# Phase 0.3 — Alert Copy Rules

Every user-facing health message must follow these rules.

## The 3-part message

1. **Pattern** — what we observed, in plain words.
2. **Uncertainty** — what we considered, what we don't know.
3. **Next action** — one clear step matching the severity level.

## Never do this

| Banned | Use instead |
|--------|-------------|
| Naming a disease ("you have…") | Describe the pattern ("higher than your usual range") |
| Certainty ("this is caused by…") | Possibility ("can temporarily increase…") |
| "Abnormal / Normal" alone | Explain what changed and over how many readings |
| Ordering medication changes | "Discuss with your clinician" |
| Claiming an exam | "Based on your device readings" |

## Per-level guidance

| Level | Tone | Must include |
|-------|------|--------------|
| L0 Informational | Friendly nudge | No action needed |
| L1 Monitor | Calm, watchful | What to watch + when to recheck |
| L2 Clinical follow-up | Serious, not alarming | Prompt to contact a clinician |
| L3 Urgent | Direct | Prompt care pathway, escalation option |
| L4 Emergency | Immediate, minimal words | Emergency number + facility option |

## Approved example (L1)

> Your heart rate has been higher than your usual range. We've noticed this pattern
> across several measurements. Recent exercise can temporarily increase heart rate,
> so we've considered your recent activity.
> What you can do: rest and recheck when relaxed. If you have concerning symptoms,
> seek medical attention. [Check again] [Contact a clinician] [Emergency help]
