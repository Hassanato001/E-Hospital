3 · Alert cards (all five levels)

PRD tone: explain the pattern, state uncertainty, recommend a next action.

L0 · Informational

Your activity was lower than your usual level today.

No concern — a gentle nudge only.

L1 · Monitor

Resting heart rate above your usual range

We've seen this across several readings. Recent exercise can raise resting HR, and we've factored in your activity. Consider monitoring it and logging any symptoms.

L2 · Clinical follow-up

Persistent change from your usual pattern

Your recent measurements differ from baseline over multiple days. This may warrant professional evaluation.

L3 · Urgent

Pattern suggests prompt medical attention

Based on validated thresholds and your profile, please seek prompt care. If symptoms worsen, escalate immediately.

L4 · Emergency

Emergency pattern detected

Call your local emergency number now. Share your emergency profile with responders if you can.

4 · Buttons

Primary for main action · Secondary for alternatives · Danger reserved for urgent/emergency · Ghost for dismissal.
